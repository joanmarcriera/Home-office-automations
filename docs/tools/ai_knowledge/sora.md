# Sora (OpenAI)

## What it is
Sora is a text-to-video AI model developed by OpenAI. It is capable of generating videos up to a minute long while maintaining high visual quality and adherence to user prompts.

## What problem it solves
It enables the creation of complex, high-fidelity video content directly from text, potentially revolutionizing video production, education, and entertainment.

## Where it fits in the stack
AI & Knowledge — Generative Media. It is a flagship large-scale generative video model.

## Typical use cases
- Generating cinematic video content from text prompts.
- Creating educational or explanatory videos.
- Prototyping visual concepts for filmmakers and designers.

## Strengths
- **Duration**: Can generate up to 60 seconds of video, significantly longer than many competitors.
- **Complexity**: Handles complex scenes with multiple characters and specific types of motion.
- **Visual Fidelity**: High resolution and stylistic consistency.

## Limitations
- **Availability**: Limited public access as of its initial announcement.
- **Safety & Ethics**: Subject to rigorous safety testing to prevent the generation of harmful content.
- **Accuracy**: May struggle with complex physics or specific cause-and-effect scenarios.

## When to use it
- For high-quality, long-form generative video content (when available).
- When complex scene composition and character consistency are required.

## When not to use it
- For tasks requiring precise, non-generative accuracy.
- If you need immediate, wide-scale public access (depending on current availability).
