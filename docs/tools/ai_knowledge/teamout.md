# TeamOut

## What it is
TeamOut is an AI agent specifically designed for the logistical planning and organization of company retreats and corporate offsites. It leverages specialized data on venues and team preferences to automate the complex coordination required for group travel.

## What problem it solves
It automates the time-consuming process of sourcing venues, managing team preferences, and coordinating the logistics of large-scale group events. It ensures that retreats meet both organizational goals and employee needs while staying within budget constraints.

## Where it fits in the stack
**AI Assistants & Knowledge / Specialized Agents**. It functions as a niche logistical operator for corporate events.

## Typical use cases
- **Venue Sourcing**: Finding offsite locations that fit specific budget and capacity requirements globally.
- **Logistics Coordination**: Managing the "moving parts" of a company retreat (transport, lodging, workspace).
- **Preference Management**: Collecting and synthesizing team feedback to choose the best retreat options.
- **Itinerary Generation**: Creating structured daily schedules including travel, work sessions, and social activities.

## Strengths
- **Domain Specialization**: Tailored specifically for the corporate retreat market with deep venue data.
- **Budget Optimization**: Iteratively adjusts retreat parameters to hit target spend without manual calculation.
- **Integrated Platform**: Works within a broader ecosystem for end-to-end event management and booking.

## Limitations
- **Niche Utility**: Limited to corporate event planning; not a general-purpose AI assistant.
- **Proprietary**: Operates as a paid service within the TeamOut managed platform.
- **Closed Ecosystem**: Integration with external non-TeamOut tools can be limited.

## When to use it
- When tasked with planning a multi-person company offsite or retreat.
- When you need to source venues that have been pre-vetted for corporate work-travel standards.
- When you want to automate the synthesis of employee dietary and activity preferences.

## When not to use it
- Not for personal travel planning or general-purpose task management.
- For small team meetings that don't require specialized venue sourcing.

## Getting started

### Account Setup
1.  Navigate to [TeamOut AI](https://app.teamout.com/ai).
2.  Create a corporate account and define your organization's general travel policies.
3.  Launch a new "Retreat Plan" and provide the "Vibe" or high-level requirements (e.g., "A relaxing beachside retreat for 30 people in September").

### Hello World (First Plan)
1.  Enter your team size and preferred regions.
2.  Allow the AI to generate an initial list of 5-10 venues.
3.  Share the preference survey generated by the agent with your team to begin ranking options.

## CLI examples

> [!NOTE]
> TeamOut is a managed, web-based platform agent. There is currently no public CLI available for interacting with the planning engine. All operations are performed via the TeamOut web dashboard.

## API examples

### JSON Request Payload
While primarily a platform-based agent, TeamOut concepts can be integrated into automation flows via structured payloads if using their enterprise API.

```json
{
  "retreat_request": {
    "team_size": 25,
    "region": "Western Europe",
    "dates": "2026-09-15 to 2026-09-19",
    "primary_goal": "Team Bonding & Strategic Planning",
    "budget_per_person": 1500,
    "requirements": [
      "High-speed Wi-Fi",
      "Private meeting rooms",
      "Vegetarian catering options",
      "Proximity to international airport"
    ]
  }
}
```
