# BigSwitch

## What it is
BigSwitch is a community-driven directory focused on European alternatives to Big Tech. It promotes digital sovereignty by highlighting GDPR-compliant, EU-owned, and often open-source tools.

## What problem it solves
It helps individuals and organizations navigate the transition from US-centric SaaS platforms to European alternatives, addressing concerns around digital sovereignty, data residency, and GDPR compliance.

## Where it fits in the stack
**Providers / Discovery Directory**. It is a selection and vetting resource for European tech providers.

## Typical use cases
- Sourcing GDPR-compliant alternatives for business communication and cloud storage
- Identifying EU-based AI and infrastructure providers
- Auditing a tech stack for digital sovereignty

## Strengths
- Focuses on digital sovereignty and EU values
- Broad directory covering multiple tech categories
- Community-driven and transparent

## Limitations
- Directory quality depends on community maintenance
- Does not provide technical integration benchmarks

## When to use it

- When seeking tech providers that operate under European jurisdiction and GDPR.
- When digital sovereignty and local data residency are priority requirements.
- For finding alternatives to dominant US-based SaaS platforms.

## When not to use it
- When you are already satisfied with your current provider and digital sovereignty is not a critical factor for the project.
- When you require extremely niche or deep technical integration that only a specific global incumbent provides.

## Key Features

- **EU-Centric**: Focuses exclusively on European technology companies and services.
- **GDPR Compliance**: Highlights tools that prioritize European data protection standards.
- **Community-Curated**: A directory built and maintained by contributors focused on digital sovereignty.
- **Open Search**: Provides a searchable index of alternatives across various categories (Cloud, Productivity, Communication, etc.).

## Getting started

Visit [bigswitch.eu](https://bigswitch.eu/) to browse the directory and discover European alternatives for your tech stack.
